# Position — Candidate side

> Owned by the candidate side. Only the candidate side revises this file. The employer
> side states its own case in [`employer.md`](./employer.md); OEP reconciles the two in
> [RECONCILIATION.md](../RECONCILIATION.md).

This is a **seed** position from the first candidate node
([resume-agent](https://github.com/yuens1002/resume-agent) /
[resume-agent-web](https://github.com/yuens1002/resume-agent-web)). It is not the final
word — it's one side's opening argument, meant to be contested and extended by other
candidates.

## What the candidate wants

1. **To be seen fairly.** Judged on the trajectory and judgment behind the work, not on
   how well a PDF was keyword-stuffed to match a posting.
2. **To own the data.** Self-hosted, self-sovereign. No platform owns the profile or
   sits between the candidate and the people querying it.
3. **To be queryable, not flattened.** A profile that answers questions and cites
   evidence — "the first reader is a model" — instead of a static document that goes
   stale the day editing stops.
4. **Honesty by construction.** Claims carry falsifiable edges (commits, tests, live
   endpoints, signed records). The candidate would rather be *checkable* than
   impressive.

## What the candidate will give in exchange

- **Verifiable claims.** Every material claim resolves to evidence via a `/verify`-style
  edge — no "trust me."
- **Progressive disclosure.** Reveal more once the employer's `/role` claims are
  themselves verified. Mutual verification before either side spends time.
- **A real node.** A running, signed agent — not a marketing page.

## What the candidate will not accept

- A central database that re-creates the walled garden under a new name.
- A verification burden that's one-directional (candidate proves everything; employer
  proves nothing).
- A standard authored top-down that encodes one party's interest as "neutral."

## Open questions the candidate brings to reconciliation

- How much to disclose, to whom, at what verified threshold?
- What's the minimal claim set an employer can require before a candidate reveals
  anything?
- How is a bad-faith employer query rate-limited or attributed without a central
  authority?
